# Sailplane Design with Semi-Monocoque Wing

This repository contains the engineering calculations, models, and documentation for the design of a sailplane featuring a semi-monocoque midwing[cite: 1]. The primary goal of this computational design project is to optimize the aircraft's aerodynamic and structural characteristics to maximize its overall range[cite: 1].

## Key Specifications

The design adheres to the following strict engineering constraints:

*   **Wingspan:** 12m to 18m[cite: 1].
*   **Fuselage Length:** Restricted to 45%–55% of the chosen wingspan[cite: 1].
*   **Weight Target:** Total aircraft weight between 250kg and 300kg[cite: 1].
*   **Weight Distribution:** A maximum of 40% of the total weight is allocated for the wing structure[cite: 1].
*   **Wing Configuration:** Midwing, dual-spar design consisting of ribs, stringers, skin, and the spar[cite: 1].
*   **Materials:** Aluminum alloy is utilized for the wing spar, ribs, and standard sheet skin, which is attached via rivets[cite: 1].
*   **Performance Limits:** Calculations are based on a wing load factor of 3.8, allowing a maximum wing tip deflection of 5% of the wingspan[cite: 1].
*   **Center of Gravity:** Initially assumed to coincide with the center of gravity of the wing[cite: 1].

## Technologies Used

*   **Computational Tools:** All structural and aerodynamic calculations must be implemented entirely within Python or MATLAB[cite: 1].

## Project Phases

The development process is structured into three distinct phases:

*   **Phase 1: Aerodynamics and Stability**
    Focuses on conceptual and preliminary design, including the coordinate system for longitudinal static stability and foundational aerodynamic calculations[cite: 1].
*   **Phase 2: Structural Design and Material Selection**
    Involves structural design, material selection, and defending aerodynamic decisions such as airfoil profile selection, fuselage selection, wing characteristics (taper and aspect ratio), drag/glider polar curves, and tail sizing[cite: 1].
*   **Phase 3: Manufacturing and Documentation**
    Culminates in an improved engineering design of the wing, 3D model designing, Design for X processes, industrial manufacturing approaches, engineering documentation, and the final evaluation report/viva[cite: 1].
